### Proposal: A PACE-Centred, Non-Redundant Column Grammar

One grammar across all views: show **stock (`LEFT`)**, **burn deviation (`PACE`)**, and **runout vs. reset (`EXH/RESET`)**, eliminating redundant intermediates and per-view synonyms.

### G1. Primaries vs. Derived
- **DECISION:** **(b) PACE-centred** — `LEFT` (%) and `PACE` (pp) are primary; `EVN` (`LEFT + PACE`) is derived (shown only as a tick on `--expand`'s bar and in JSON).
- **PURPOSE:** Give the owner's control variable (`PACE`) primacy without spending width on redundant arithmetic.
- **EVIDENCE:** `PACE = EVN - LEFT` fixes three numbers with two degrees of freedom. Every decision rule—balance (`cc <= ag == cx`), bands (`-10`, `+20`), colour, and `gap vs X-7D (pp)`—is stated in `PACE`, never `EVN`.
- **REASON:** Dropping `/EVN` saves 3–4 columns per row right where `EXH/RESET` (+5–7 chars) and `: p1,p2` push rows against the 80-col limit.
- **Rejected:** **(a) `LEFT/EVN` + `PACE`** wastes width on an intermediate with no threshold; **(c) `LEFT/EVN` only** forces mental subtraction to check bands/balance; **(d) `USED/ELAPSED`** hides remaining quota.
- **Effects:** *Compact/Expand*: `LEFT` + `PACE` (`EVN` tick on expand bar). *diag --plan*: unchanged primaries (`LEFT`, `PACE`). *JSON*: emits `left_pct`, `even_left_pct`, `pace_pp`.

### G2. Pairing Grammar
- **DECISION:** Slash pairs always mean **`actual/ref`** (projected or observed value / schedule reference), suffixed `!` iff `actual < ref` breaches the reference. Apply **only** when the delta is not already its own column: `EXH/RESET` uses it; `LEFT` does not (`PACE` is its delta); the gap line is a signed `ΔPACE` list (`gap vs X-7D (pp): C-7D +2, A-7D +20`), not a slash pair.
- **PURPOSE:** One unambiguous reading of `/` without visual clutter.
- **EVIDENCE:** `20h/1d10h!` directly encodes both durations and the breach condition without needing a fourth duration-delta column.
- **REASON:** Consistent left-to-right comparison (`my trajectory / limit`) prevents reversing numerator/denominator across columns.
- **Rejected:** Pairing everything (`LEFT/EVN`, `EXH/RESET`, `C/X` gaps)—duplicates `PACE` and obscures `pp` magnitudes.
- **Effects:** *Compact/Expand/diag --plan*: `EXH/RESET` replaces `RESET`/`RESET-IN`/`RESETS`. *JSON*: `exhaust_in_seconds`, `resets_in_seconds`, `exhaust_before_reset`.

### G3. Names, Order, and Units

# Round 1: Unified PACE-Centred Column Grammar

## G1: Primary vs. Derived Numbers
* **DECISION**: **(b) Simplified PACE-centred** — `LEFT` and `PACE` are the two independent primary table numbers; `EVN` is derived (`LEFT + PACE`) and relegated to the visual bar tick and JSON (`even_left_pct`).
* **PURPOSE**: Eliminate redundant columns on 80-column screens while directly displaying the metric governing all operational rules.
* **EVIDENCE**: Identity `PACE = EVN - LEFT` means showing `LEFT/EVN` alongside `PACE` prints three values for two degrees of freedom. Both balance targets (`cc <= ag == cx`) and health bands (`[-10, +20]`) are defined in `PACE`.
* **REASON**: Showing `17/21%` and `+4` forces the reader to mentally verify subtraction on every row and costs 3–4 extra characters per row. `LEFT` answers "how much fuel is in the tank?"; `PACE` answers "am I ahead or behind schedule?".
* **REJECTED**: **(a) `LEFT/EVN` + `PACE`** — violates 80-col economy by duplicating information and muddies whether `EVN` or `PACE` is the primary control reference.
* **EFFECT**: Compact/Expand/Plan drop `EVN` from text cells (`17%`, not `17/21%`). Expand `[BAR]` renders the `EVN` marker tick visually. JSON keeps `left_pct`, `pace_pt`, and `even_left_pct`.

## G2: Pairing Grammar
* **DECISION**: Strict **`ACTUAL/REF[flag]`** grammar (`my projected or current value / steady-state benchmark`), applied solely to **`EXH/RESET`** (`20h/1d10h!`) in table cells, and mirrored structurally in the summary line `gap vs X-7D (pp): C-7D +2, A-7D +20`.
* **PURPOSE**: Establish a single mental model whenever a slash or comparison appears: *left of slash = current trajectory; right of slash = schedule boundary*.
* **EVIDENCE**: In `20h/1d10h!`, `20h` is actual time-to-exhaustion at current burn and `1d10h` is the reset horizon; `!` flags `ACTUAL < REF`.
* **REASON**: Because G1 folds `LEFT/EVN` into `LEFT` and `PACE` (`ACTUAL - REF`), the table needs only one slash-pair column (`EXH/RESET`), preventing "slash fatigue".
* **REJECTED**: **Two pair columns (`LEFT/EVN` and `EXH/RESET`)** — creates visual clutter and mixes an additive identity (`EVN - LEFT = PACE`) with a ratio hazard (`EXH < RESET -> !`).
* **EFFECT**: Compact and Expand show `EXH/RESET`. Plan uses `EXH/RESET` too (replacing single `RESETS`). JSON unchanged.

## G3: Vocabulary, Order, and PACE Format
* **DECISION**:
  * **Vocabulary**: `STATE`, `LEFT`, `PACE`, `EXH/RESET`, `FAMILY` (monitor) and `LEFT`, `PACE`, `EXH/RESET`, `ALLOW%/h`, `ACTION`, `REASON` (`diag --plan`).
  * **Order**: `PACE` sits immediately after `LEFT` and before `EXH/RESET`.
  * **Units & Format**: Header carries units only when non-obvious; `LEFT` prints `%` in cells (`17%`), `PACE` prints `[glyph][±]int` in monitor (`^+4`, `v-12`) and `[±]0.0` in plan (`+3.8`), with unit `pp` declared in the legend (no per-cell `pp` suffix).
* **PURPOSE**: Unify the three views (`LEFT` everywhere, never `REM`; `EXH/RESET` everywhere, never `RESET-IN`/`RESETS`) and group capacity state (`LEFT`, `PACE`) before time horizon (`EXH/RESET`).
* **EVIDENCE**: Today's codebase uses three names for remaining time (`RESET`, `RESET-IN`, `RESETS`) and three formats for `PACE` (`^+4`, `^+3.8pp`, `+3.8`).
* **REASON**: Placing `PACE` right after `LEFT` pairs *stock* (`17%`) with *burn delta* (`^+4`), which together explain *time consequence* (`20h/1d10h!`). Integer `PACE` in monitor saves 4 columns over `^+3.8pp`; 1-decimal in `--plan` preserves precision for AI planning without glyph noise.
* **REJECTED**: **Per-cell `pp` suffix (`^+3.8pp`) in monitor** — wastes 4 columns per row with static characters and forces right-hand column drops on 80-col terminals.
* **EFFECT**: All three views adopt `LEFT`, `PACE`, `EXH/RESET` in identical order.

## G4: `TARGET%/h` Role and Naming
* **DECISION**: Keep in `diag --plan` only, renamed to **`ALLOW%/h`** (`left% / hours_to_reset`). Omit from monitor.
* **PURPOSE**: Distinguish *historical burn offset* (`PACE`) from *future sustainable burn rate* (`ALLOW%/h`) for AI scheduling.
* **EVIDENCE**: `EXH/RESET` measures *current* velocity to exhaustion, whereas `left% / reset_h` prescribes the *future* ceiling rate needed to land at 0% exactly at reset.
* **REASON**: Folding it into `EXH/RESET` loses the actionable rate limit an AI agent needs to throttle tasks. Renaming `TARGET%/h` to `ALLOW%/h` avoids confusion with the owner's balance targets (`cc <= ag == cx`).
* **REJECTED**: **Showing `ALLOW%/h` in human monitor or folding into `EXH/RESET`** — humans steer by `PACE` bands and `EXH/RESET!`; AI planners need the numeric hourly rate budget.
* **EFFECT**: Compact/Expand omit it. `diag --plan` renames header `TARGET%/h` -> `ALLOW%/h`. JSON keeps `target_pct_per_hour` + adds alias `allow_pct_per_hour`.

## G5: Compact vs. Expand Parity
* **DECISION**: **Strict cell parity.** Expand uses the exact same column names and cell formats as Compact (`STATE LEFT PACE EXH/RESET`), adding `[BAR]` before `FAMILY` when terminal width > 90 (or replacing `FAMILY` suffix details at 80 cols) plus sub-pool/profile rows.

* **EVIDENCE**: Decision record mandates compact POOLS and `--expand` QUOTA change identically; previously expand diverged (`REM`, `RESET-IN`, `^+3.8pp`, omitted `EXHAUST-IN` and `PROFILES`).
* **REASON**: If a cell changes format (`^+4` -> `^+3.8pp`) or columns vanish (`EXH/RESET` or profile IDs) on `--expand`, `--expand` becomes a different tool rather than a drill-down.
* **REJECTED**: **Expand having its own decimal `PACE` or dropping `EXH`/`PROFILES` for `[BAR]`** — breaks parity and hides critical exhaustion warnings in expanded view.
* **EFFECT**: Compact and Expand share one row formatter; Expand only unlocks hidden child rows and appends `[BAR]` when width permits.

## G6: JSON Naming Consistency
* **DECISION**: Standardize on orthogonal unit suffixes: **`_pct`** (0–100%), **`_pp`** (percentage points delta), **`_pct_per_hour`** (rate), **`_in_seconds`** (duration), and **`bool` verb phrases**. Keep all legacy keys and add `_pp` alias.
* **PURPOSE**: Make units self-describing for consumers without breaking existing parsers.
* **EVIDENCE**: Existing `pace_pt` uses `_pt` while text uses `pp` (percentage points), and `resets_in_seconds` vs `exhaust_in_seconds` differ only in verb plurality.
* **REASON**: Additive aliases (`pace_pp` alongside `pace_pt`; `reset_in_seconds` alongside `resets_in_seconds`; `allow_pct_per_hour` alongside `target_pct_per_hour`) achieve a clean schema (`left_pct`, `even_left_pct`, `pace_pp`, `exhaust_in_seconds`, `reset_in_seconds`, `exhaust_before_reset`, `allow_pct_per_hour`) with zero breakage.
* **REJECTED**: **Renaming `pace_pt` or `resets_in_seconds` in-place** — breaks existing callers and violates the additive-only constraint.
* **EFFECT**: JSON output emits both canonical and legacy keys.

## G7: Single-Source Legend Line
* **DECISION**: One 78-char legend line printed directly under the table header in `monitor` (and imported verbatim into docs/help):
  `LEFT=rem%  PACE=used-elap(pp): <=-10 spend | ok | >+20 rest  EXH/RESET: !=exh<rst`

* **EVIDENCE**: Contains the exact formula (`used-elap(pp)`), band thresholds (`<=-10`, `>+20`), and `!` rule (`exh<rst`) within 80 columns.
* **REASON**: Keeping a single constant string in code and testing docs against it guarantees monitor and docs never drift.
* **REJECTED**: **Multi-line footer or doc-only legend** — wastes vertical screen real estate or forces users to open docs to learn what `^+4` and `!` mean.
