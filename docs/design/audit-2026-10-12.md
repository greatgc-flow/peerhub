## 6. The 5-Whys Rules
Lessons derived from past incidents require strict adherence to five engineering rules:
1. **Test emitted bytes**: Assert exact transmitted bytes and escape codes at the terminal boundary; never trust in-memory models alone (from ESC/#80 literal redraw bug).
2. **Claims need measured validation**: Empirical claims require production sample validation; do not substitute synthetic formulas or unanchored models (from `recent_rate` forecast bug).
3. **Expose the safe next action**: Operational errors and uncertain states must instruct the user on the precise safe recovery path (from `blocked_uncertain` stuck-stream deadlock).
4. **Explicit store ownership**: Database locations and provenance must be deterministic and explicit; forbid ambient subfolder auto-discovery (from wrong cwd / ambient discovery divergence).
5. **Disposable declared environments**: Isolate tools and runners; rely only on declared, pinned dependencies in disposable virtual environments (from shared venv drift).
